\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
\paragraph{Benchmark.} HM3D ObjectNav \citep{hm3d}, Habitat-Sim v0.2.1
\citep{habitat}, \textsc{val\_mini} split (30 episodes), six goal categories
(bed, chair, sofa, toilet, tv\_monitor, plant). Success threshold
$1.0$\,m geodesic, max $500$ steps.

\paragraph{Shared infrastructure.} All methods use the Co-NavGPT perception
stack (Detectron2 + RedNet + 2D semantic map + FMM planner + frontier
extraction every $25$ steps) with $K{=}2$ cooperative robots. Only the
frontier-assignment module differs.

\paragraph{Hardware.} 4$\times$ RTX 4090 (24\,GB). Habitat sim on GPU 0+1,
models on GPU 2+3.

\paragraph{Models.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item MindNav brain: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (local) or MiniMax-M2.5 (API).
    \item Co-NavGPT brain: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (same weights).
    \item MCoCoNav: Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct.
    \item EfficientNav: Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct (adapted from LLaVA-34B
          upstream).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Findings}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{VLM spatial reasoning collapses at 7B.} Both VLM variants
    reach $0\%$ despite using exploration infrastructure that achieves $76\%$
    with random assignment. The interface, not the exploration loop, is the
    binding constraint.
    \item \textbf{KG tool-calling outperforms flat text.} MindNav improves SR
    from $0.700\rightarrow 0.733$ ($+3.3$ absolute) and SPL from
    $0.306\rightarrow 0.416$ ($+36\%$ relative) at identical perception,
    planner, and LLM scale.
    \item \textbf{Random is strong.} Two robots with Co-NavGPT's perception
    and FMM already cover most of \textsc{val\_mini}, so SR ceilings are
    close. SPL --- path efficiency --- is where KG reasoning pays off.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Episode Breakdown}
Episodes 4--30 (27 eps, resume run): 21 succ / 6 fail
($\mathrm{SR}{=}0.778$, $\mathrm{SPL}{=}0.418$).
Episodes 1--3 (rerun): 1 succ, 2 fail (Ep.~1 DTG $2.5$\,m, Ep.~3 DTG
$11.0$\,m). Combined: $22/30 = 0.733$.

\subsection{Ablations}
\paragraph{KG edges.} Removing \textsc{connected\_to} edges (leaving only
containment and proximity) drops MindNav to $\mathrm{SR}{=}0.667$ by pushing
robots into wall-separated rooms when choosing frontiers.
\paragraph{Certainty aggregation.} Replacing noisy-OR with max-confidence
reduces SR by $\sim 0.03$ on categories with multiple partial detections
(plant, tv\_monitor). The effect is small but consistent.
